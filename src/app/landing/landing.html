ABOUT US

Making Barangay Services Easier for Everyone

MankilamEase is the Barangay Mankilam Service System designed to make accessing barangay services more convenient and organized.

Instead of spending unnecessary time waiting at the barangay office, residents can submit document requests, monitor their requests, and stay updated with barangay announcements through the online system.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple Steps to Get Your Document

Requesting a barangay document is simple. Follow these steps using the online service system.

01

Create an Account

Register your resident account and provide the required information to get started.

02

Submit a Request

Choose the document you need, provide the purpose, and submit your request online.

03

Track Your Request

Monitor your request as it moves through the barangay's processing stages.

04

Pick Up Your Document

Once your request is ready, visit the barangay office to claim your document.
